Privacy policy

Starhive Defense · effective November 1, 2026

What we collect

Nothing. Starhive Defense does not collect, store, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your best score is saved only on your iPhone so the game can show it next time. It never leaves your device, and deleting the app deletes it.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the new version of the app is released.
