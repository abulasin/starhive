How to play

Drag anywhere on the screen to steer your cannon, and keep your finger down to fire. Catch falling boxes for power-ups: T gives a triple shot, R gives rapid fire, and S gives a shield that blocks one hit. Tap the screen once to turn sound on.

Something not working?

Open an issue on this project's GitHub page and describe what happened, which iPhone you use, and your iOS version. We read every report.
